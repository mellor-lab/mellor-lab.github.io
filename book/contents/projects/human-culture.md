# Human slice work from resected brain tissue

This project is led by [Shyline Bajaba](../our-team/members/shylinebajaba), [Matt Claydon](../our-team/members/mattclaydon) and our collaborators at North Bristol NHS Trust and University Hospitals Bristol and Weston NHS Foundation Trust. We use cultured organotypic slices prepared from resected human brain tissue to investigate how altered expression of high-risk genes for schizophrenia affects. cellular physiology, synaptic properties, and dendritic integration.


### Aim:

::::{grid} 1 2 2 2
:padding: 0

:::{grid-item}

We aim to determine how downregulating high-risk schizophrenia susceptibility genes impairs cellular and synaptic physiology in a human brain slice model.

### Techniques:
Organotypic culturing methods combined with acute brain slice preparations, whole-cell patch clamp electrophysiology, and 2-photon imaging of dendritic calcium.
