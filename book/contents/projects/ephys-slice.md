# Investigating mechanisms in brain slices

This project, led by [Matt Claydon](../our-team/members/mattclaydon), aims to investigate the changes to cellular physiology induced by altered expression schizophrenia risk genes and the underlying mechanisms in brain slices. 



### Aim:

::::{grid} 1 2 2 2
:padding: 0

:::{grid-item}

We aim to characterize how schizophrenia risk genes change physiology on a cellular level, ranging from intrinsic neuronal properties and morphology to synaptic transmission and dendritic integration.

### Techniques:
We utilise whole-cell patch clamp electrophysiology and ex-vivo 2-photon calcium imaging.
